Time Sensei

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026 · Türkçe

Time Sensei is a timing game. This policy describes exactly what the app collects, why, and who else can see it. It is written against what the app actually does — every item below corresponds to real code and a real database table.

Contact: kmertcansdev@gmail.com

The short version

  • You can play without creating an account or giving us your name or email.
  • We collect your scores, so your history and rankings work.
  • We collect crash reports, so the app can be fixed when it breaks.
  • There are no ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and your data is never sold or given to data brokers or advertisers.

What the app collects

An anonymous account

The first time you open Time Sensei it creates an anonymous account automatically. This is a random identifier — not your name, email, phone number, or Apple ID, unless you choose to sign in. Stored with it: a randomly generated display name, the creation date, and your chosen app language.

Why: so your scores belong to something, and so your progress can survive a reinstall if you later sign in.

Your scores

Every completed round records the game mode, the score, and how far off the target you were, with a timestamp. Survival runs additionally record how many rounds you survived and your average error.

Why: rankings and your own performance history.

Crash and error reports

When something goes wrong, the app sends a diagnostic report containing the error, a stack trace, and basic technical context such as your device model and operating system version. These are processed by Sentry.

Why: so faults get found and fixed. Crash reports are not used to build a profile of you.

Notification token

If you turn on notifications, iOS issues a token that lets us send you a push message. It identifies the installation, not you. If notifications are off, no token is stored.

If you choose to sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional and exists only so your progress survives changing or resetting your device. If you use it, Apple sends us an account identifier and, depending on the choice you make in Apple's own dialog, either your email address or a private relay address. You can play the whole game without ever doing this.

Referrals

If you invite a friend and they accept, we store the link between the two accounts so both sides receive the reward.

What stays only on your device

The following never leaves your phone. It lives in the app's local storage and is deleted when you delete the app:

  • Your rank and streak progress
  • Which modes you have unlocked and how many rounds you have played in each
  • Your best timing error per mode
  • Achievements
  • Daily quests
  • Time IQ results and history
  • Theme and sound preferences

Each is used strictly to operate the app. None is given your data for their own advertising or profiling.

Tracking and advertising

Time Sensei contains no advertising and no third-party tracking or analytics SDKs. Nothing in the app tracks you across other companies' apps or websites, and the app does not ask permission to do so, because it does not do it.

How long data is kept

Scores and account records are kept while your account exists. Crash reports are kept for a limited retention window by Sentry and then deleted automatically.

Your choices

  • Play anonymously. Do not sign in, and nothing here is connected to a real identity.
  • Turn off notifications in the app's settings, or in iOS Settings, and no token is stored.
  • Delete everything. In the app: Settings → Delete Account, which removes your account and its scores from the server. You can also write to the address above to request it. Deleting the app removes all on-device data immediately.

Children

Time Sensei is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. Nothing in the app asks a player their age, because the app has no reason to know it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes rather than made quietly.
